# RB-10: Water Leak Detected

## Symptoms
- Leak detection alarm from a sensor cable or spot sensor under a raised floor or near cooling equipment.
- Water visible on the floor, drip from piping, or a wet ceiling tile.
- Falling chilled water pressure or a rising makeup water flow.
- Drain pan overflow alarm on a cooling unit.

## Immediate actions
- Locate the sensor zone and send someone to inspect the area.
- Keep people and equipment away from standing water near power.
- Isolate the water source if it is known and safe to do so.
- Move equipment out of the wet area if it is safe.
- Notify facilities and the site manager.

## Diagnosis
- Trace the piping and the cooling units near the alarming sensor.
- Check the drain pans, condensate lines, and humidifier supply for overflow or blockage.
- Compare chilled water loop pressure and makeup flow to normal.
- Check whether the leak is clean water, glycol, or something else.
- Review sensor history for a slow or sudden onset.

## Recovery
- Stop the leak at the source: close the valve, or isolate the section of piping.
- Dry the area and check nearby power and cable pathways before restoring power.
- Repair the pipe, fitting, or drain and refill and vent the loop if required.
- Confirm sensors are dry and clear the alarm.

## Escalation
- Escalate immediately to facilities mechanical and the site manager for any active leak.
- Call the electrical on-call if water reaches power equipment.
- Notify the IT owners of racks that may be affected.
