# RB-07: High Humidity

## Symptoms
- Relative humidity or dew point above the allowable range in the data hall.
- Humidity alarm from room sensors or a cooling unit; condensation risk on cold surfaces.
- Humidifier stuck on, or a cooling coil running too cold.
- Rising humidity after a door or containment breach, or a wet-weather outside air intrusion.

## Immediate actions
- Confirm the reading with a second sensor.
- Close open doors, panels, and any breaches to outside air.
- Check whether a humidifier is running and turn it off if the humidity is high.
- Check whether the dew point is near rack inlet temperature.
- Log readings every few minutes.

## Diagnosis
- Compare humidity and dew point across room sensors to find the area affected.
- Check the cooling coil valve position and the chilled water temperature.
- Check the humidifier status, water supply, and control setpoint.
- Look for a water source: a leak, a wet floor, or a flooded drain pan.
- Check the makeup air or economizer dampers.

## Recovery
- Correct the cause: reset the humidifier, adjust the setpoint, or seal the breach.
- Let the room return to range gradually and confirm dew point is safe.
- Clear the alarm and record the event.

## Escalation
- Escalate to facilities if humidity stays high for 30 minutes.
- Escalate to the site manager if condensation is seen on equipment.
- Call the cooling vendor for a repeated humidifier or sensor fault.
